History

Dry Oar Rafting was founded to bring adventure to everyone. We started with just one boat and a passion for the river. Over the years, we have expanded our fleet and our team, but our core mission remains the same: providing safe, thrilling, and memorable experiences on the water for all skill levels.

Adventure Awaits You!

Join us for an exciting rafting experience guided by professionals who prioritize your safety and your fun. From calm scenic floats to heart-pounding rapids, we offer trips that cater to everyone.
